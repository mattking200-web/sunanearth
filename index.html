The big ideas! 💡

Why does day and night happen?

Earth spins around like a top, once every day. The side facing the Sun gets daytime. The side facing away gets nighttime.

Why does Niagara have seasons?

Earth leans a little to one side. As it flies around the Sun all year, that lean changes how much sunshine Niagara gets.

Why are summer days longer?

In summer, Niagara leans toward the Sun. That means longer days and a Sun that climbs high in the sky.

Why are winter days shorter?

In winter, Niagara leans away from the Sun. That means shorter days and a Sun that stays low in the sky.
