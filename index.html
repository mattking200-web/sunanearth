Key Observation

Earth spins around like a globe on a stand! The side facing the Sun gets light and experiences DAY. The side turned away is in shadow and experiences NIGHT.

Axial Tilt: 23.5° Tilted Stand
Pin Location: Welland, ON (Canada)
Full Rotation: 24 Hours (1 Day)

Activity 2: Ground View in Welland

🌅 Time: 06:00 AM (Morning)
Time of Day Control 12:00 PM (Noon)
🌅

Apparent Motion

The Sun seems to rise in the EAST in the morning, climb high at noon, and set in the WEST in the evening. But remember: the Sun isn't moving around us—Earth is spinning!

☀️ Morning: Sun rises in the East.
🌤️ Afternoon: Sun is highest in the sky.
🌙 Night: We face away from the Sun into dark space.

Why Seasons Change

Earth takes 1 year (365 days) to travel around the Sun. Because Earth's axis is tilted at 23.5°, Welland tilts TOWARD the Sun in Summer (direct light, long days) and AWAY in Winter (indirect light, short days).

Tilted Toward
❄️ Winter
Tilted Away
